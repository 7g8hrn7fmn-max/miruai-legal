Common Questions

How do I restore my subscription?

Open MiruAI, go to Settings, and tap "Restore Purchases".

How accurate are the nutrition estimates?

MiruAI uses AI to estimate nutritional content from photos. Results are approximations and may vary depending on portion size, preparation method, and ingredients. They should not be used as a substitute for professional dietary advice.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings → Reset App Data to clear all scan history and profile information from your device.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through Apple. Go to your iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions to cancel.
